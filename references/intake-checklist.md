# Intake Checklist

Extract these from what the user has provided before asking. Ask only for gaps that change the recommendation.

## Per adult applicant
1. Nationality / passport(s), including any second nationality or ancestry claims (e.g. a parent or grandparent born in an EU country)
2. Current country of residence and current immigration status there (e.g. employment visa, golden visa, dependant)
3. Date of birth: compute the exact age now and the date of the next age-points drop
4. Marital status; spouse/partner details (assess the spouse as a potential main applicant too)
5. Dependants: children's ages and any other dependants
6. Education: degrees, institutions, years, grades; whether degrees are likely to be recognised (e.g. Anabin/ZAB, WES/ECA, ACS/VETASSESS)
7. Profession, occupation codes (NOC / ANZSCO / SOC / ISCO), and years of skilled experience *after* the qualifying degree
8. Current employment status and employer type
9. Income (gross, fixed vs variable, since many thresholds ignore bonuses)
10. Savings and liquid assets (proof-of-funds requirements)
11. Business ownership
12. Investment capacity
13. Language ability: actual test scores (IELTS/PTE/CELPIP/TEF/Goethe), versus self-assessed CV levels
14. Publications, awards, patents, media (relevant for talent and self-petition routes)
15. Travel history (prior visas to the US/UK/Schengen/Canada/Australia help)
16. Previous visa refusals anywhere (must be disclosed on most forms)
17. Immigration violations or overstays
18. Criminal history, only where legally relevant
19. Medical issues, only where relevant to admissibility, and only if the client raises them

## Goals and constraints
20. Intended destination(s), or openness to any
21. Purpose: work, study, family, business, lifestyle
22. Desired timeline
23. Long-term objective: temporary residence, PR, or citizenship; and whether dual citizenship matters
24. Ability to relocate physically (presence requirements); willingness to live regionally or learn a language
25. Budget for the process itself

## Document checklist (typical; tailor to the route)
- Passports (valid well beyond the intended move) and national ID
- Degree certificates and transcripts; credential evaluation or recognition reports
- Experience reference letters on letterhead, with duties, dates, hours, and salary (from every relevant employer)
- Payslips, employment contracts, tax records
- Language test results
- Police clearance certificates from every country of residence (often 6+ months of residence), which usually have long lead times
- Marriage certificate; children's birth certificates
- Bank statements / proof of funds
- Medical examinations (panel physicians where required)
- CV in the destination's preferred format (e.g. Europass for EU)
- For researchers: publication list, ORCID, recommendation letters, PhD offer or funding letter
